Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Dash of cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients until smooth.
  2. Heat a skillet and pour batter into small rounds.
  3. Flip when bubbles form and cook the other side until golden.
