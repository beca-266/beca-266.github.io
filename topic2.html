    • Creating text that fluidly adapts to various screen sizes and devices is known as responsive typography. Whether viewed on a small smartphone screen or a large desktop monitor, it guarantees that fonts stay readable and aesthetically pleasing. This entails applying media queries to alter font styles, sizes, or spacing according to the viewport and substituting relative units such as em, rem, or vw for fixed pixel sizes. In addition to improving accessibility and user experience, well-designed responsive typography also adds to a polished, businesslike online appearance.
