about cookies...

  • Cookies are tiny data files that websites save on a user's device in order to store information about their visit. They are frequently used to track user behavior for analytics or advertising, store preferences, and preserve user sessions. Cookies can, for instance, remember items in a shopping cart, keep a user logged in, or customize content based on previous interactions. A lot of websites now have cookie consent banners and adhere to laws like the GDPR to make sure users are aware of and have control over how their data is used, even though cookies improve user experience but also raise privacy concerns.
